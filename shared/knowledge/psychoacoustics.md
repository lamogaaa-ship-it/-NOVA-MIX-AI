# Psychoacoustics for mixing decisions

## Equal loudness
The ear is most sensitive around 2-5 kHz; small boosts there are very audible and fatiguing on loud passages. Low and very high frequencies need more energy to be perceived at the same loudness, more so at low playback levels.

## Masking
Louder sounds mask quieter ones nearby in frequency, especially upward in frequency. Mud (200-500 Hz) masks intelligibility; reverb masks direct sound.

## A/B honesty
Always compare at equal loudness. Listen to the difference signal (delta) to hear exactly what processing changed. Short, repeated A/B switches are more reliable than long listening.
