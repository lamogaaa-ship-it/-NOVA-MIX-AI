# Creative effects

## Rhythmic gating and stutter
A tempo-synced gate chops the signal on musical divisions (1/8, 1/16, triplets). Patterns such as 3-3-2 (tresillo) or broken sixteenths sound less mechanical than straight chops. Short fades (1-5 ms) on each edge avoid clicks. The effect must follow the host tempo and transport position.

## Saturation flavours
Tape: symmetric soft saturation, gentle compression of peaks, warmth. Tube: asymmetric, even harmonics, richness. Soft clip: denser, more aggressive, adds odd harmonics. Always compare saturation loudness matched, because saturation raises perceived loudness.

## Width tricks
Short delays or ping-pong delays on the returns add width while keeping the lead centred and mono compatible.
