# Reference matching

## What to match
Match perceptual characteristics, not samples: tonal balance (broad, smoothed spectrum), dynamics (crest factor, loudness range, phrase consistency), space (tail/decay), width (side/mid energy), colour/density, and loudness. Compare loudness-matched spectra (relative to total energy) so that level differences do not masquerade as tone differences.

## Pitfalls
Different notes and arrangements change the fine spectrum; match smoothed tonal balance, not note-level peaks. A full-mix reference is not a vocal reference: without separating the vocal, only broad mid/high balance is comparable. A reference drier than the recording cannot be matched by removing baked-in reverb. Matching should be iterative: process, measure again, refine.

## Influence
Matching 100% of the difference is rarely musical. An influence control (0-100%) scales how far to move toward the reference.
